## 1. THE CHANGE

- **The rule** (`SoftFloat.f64to32`, SDM Vol. 2A CVTSD2SS):
  - **A NaN** keeps its sign and the top 23 bits of its fraction, and is quieted; the low 29 payload bits are dropped,
    never rounded. IE on an SNaN.
  - **±∞ and ±0** convert exactly and raise nothing.
  - **Otherwise** the value is rounded by `roundPack` at binary32: OE with PE on overflow, UE with PE when inexact and
    tiny AFTER rounding, PE when inexact.
  - **DE on a binary64 denormal**, beside the UE and PE its rounding raises.
  - Three processors read every one of these before the batch (D272).
- **The write** (`vcvtsd2ssLow`): the destination's low 32 bits; bits 127:32 are kept, and the old low lane is not an input.
- **Two constructors, not a fifth `VArithOp` member** (D273 §1): the fold's premise, one operand shape, does not hold.
- **`wrongSimdWith` runs the true rule for the two new constructors,** so only B0's `raise`-swapping arm reaches them.
