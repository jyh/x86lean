# P2 BATCH 41 — sub-group B0: MXCSR in the record, and the flags of every landed FP form

> ⚠️ **TWO COUNTERS.** Twenty-seventh differential record; the seat's **batch 41** in `docs/DECISIONS.md`
> (D267, designed in D266). QUEUE `P2-NEXT (B)`, built and run on 2026-09-17.

**Forms.** **No new instruction and no new roster row.** One state field (`Cpu.mxcsr`), the sticky exception flags of
the 14 landed FP mnemonics, and a record that carries those flags. The mnemonics are `comiss` · `comisd` · `ucomiss` ·
`ucomisd` · `minss` · `minsd` · `maxss` · `maxsd` · `minps` · `maxps` · `cvtss2sd` · `cvtsi2sdl` · `cvttsd2si` ·
`cvttss2si`. This is the first batch of the soft-float commission's sub-group B (D139), and its harness. `mulsd`/`mulss`
are B1.
⚠️ "A′" below is the CI job's ku arm (`ku-delta --arm a-prime`), not the sub-group.

## 1. THE CHANGE

- **The state.** `Cpu.mxcsr : BitVec 32 := 0x1F80`, the power-up value. A landed FP form ORs the flags it raises into
  the six sticky bits (`Cpu.withSimd`). A raised flag that is UNMASKED makes the step refuse, and no pre-state unmasks
  one.
- **The flags,** from the SDM's per-instruction exception lists:
  - COMIS raises IE on any NaN, and UCOMIS only on an SNaN;
  - MIN/MAX raise IE on any NaN;
  - `cvtss2sd` raises IE on an SNaN;
  - `cvtts?2si` raises IE or PE;
  - DE is raised on a denormal operand, and never when an operand is a NaN.
- **The record** prints `mxcsr.ie= … mxcsr.pe=` and `mxcsr.ctl=` on both sides, one key per flag.
  `scripts/check_mxcsr_format.py` holds the two literals together.
- **The driver** loads `:mxcsr` on every case, so a flag raised by one case cannot leak into the next (D265 §4).
- **The pre-states** carry MXCSR by index:
  - RC = i mod 4;
  - the sticky bits are preset on half the states, and OE is never preset (D266 §4);
  - every exception is masked;
  - DAZ and FZ are clear.
  **Every earlier run held RC and the sticky bits at their power-up values**, so this batch re-tests every landed FP
  form (D27).

## 4. ⛔ WHERE THE DIFFERENTIAL CANNOT CARRY IT — PINNED IN THE KERNEL

Five `decide`s in `Tests/Anchors.lean` run `step` on 40 of `hwprobe/`'s 89 non-multiply rows.
- **The block is generated by `hwprobe/mk_anchors.py` from `mk_rows.py`'s rules,** and `--check` holds the file to it.
- **A row is pinned when either holds:**
  - x86isa disagrees with it or cannot run it (20);
  - no vector of its form reaches its class over the 88 pre-states (20).
  That covers:
  - COMIS at a QNaN, and every SNaN at a comparison or at min;
  - a denormal beside a QNaN at `ucomis`, and equality where no vector compares a register with itself;
  - `cvtss2sd` at −0 and under a preset OE;
  - integer 0 at round-down, and two (value, RC) pairs;
  - `cvttsd2si` at an SNaN, at exactly INT_MIN, and at an exact in-range value.
- **x86isa on all 89:** it runs 88 and disagrees on 19, in three mechanisms (COMIS at a QNaN 6 · preset OE 12 · integer 0
  → −0 at round-down 1). All 19 are pinned.
- **Planted wrong once each:** five plants gave five failures, one per theorem.
- ⛔ **All 89 rows were pinned first, and the A′ gate refused it** (`Tests.Anchors` +68,196 ku against 60,901). The 49
  unpinned rows are reached by vectors that x86isa agrees with. The 40 read 31,229 kernel unfoldings. See D267 §2.
- ⇒ **"COMIS runs as UCOMIS" is refuted here and nowhere else.** The oracle agrees with that wrong model, so no vector
  can.
